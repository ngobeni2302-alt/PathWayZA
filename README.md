## 📖 About PathwayZA

PathwayZA is an innovative career guidance platform built for primary, high school, and college students in South Africa. It empowers students to map their school subjects, personal interests, and existing qualifications to real, relevant, and high-demand career paths.

We treat all qualification routes—including university degrees, TVET college diplomas, apprenticeships, learnerships, and internships—with equal weight. Our insights are backed by South African job market data, salary benchmarks, funding diagnostics, and an intelligent career scoring algorithm.

## 🌟 Core Features

- **🎓 Intelligent Subject-to-Career Matcher**: Computes real-time career suitability scores based on subject selections, degrees, and interest profiles, respecting South African university admission constraints.
- **🔍 South African Career Explorer**: A searchable, filterable database of 30+ major local careers showcasing live market demand, average salaries, and qualification routes.
- **💰 Bursaries & Funding Finder**: A dedicated hub to explore corporate and SETA bursaries to help fund your education journey.
- **🛠️ Artisan & Work-Based Opportunities**: Direct links to apprenticeships, learnerships, and internships across South Africa.
- **📈 5-10 Year Career Trend Forecasting**: Visualizes local sector growth projections (Tech, Green Energy, Health, Finance, Engineering) using economic insights.
- **✅ Accreditation Validator**: Scans institution profiles against a simulated DHET/SAQA database of public universities and TVET colleges to safeguard students from bogus academies.
- **🤖 AI Career Assistant**: Contextualized advice regarding study roadmaps and bursaries, powered by an intelligent response system.
- **🔐 Ecosystem Integration**: Seamlessly integrates across our digital ecosystem, sharing your profile and subject list to connect you directly with job boards and certification hubs.

## 🚀 Quick Start Guide

PathwayZA is powered by a modern **React frontend** built with **Vite** for incredibly fast local development and instant hot module replacement.

### Running Locally

1. **Clone & Navigate** to the project directory:
   ```bash
   cd /path/to/PathWayZA
   ```

2. **Install Dependencies**:
   ```bash
   npm install
   ```

3. **Start the Development Server** (bound to your local network):
   ```bash
   npm run dev -- --host 0.0.0.0
   ```

4. **Open your browser** and navigate to the Local or Network URL provided in your terminal (usually `http://localhost:5173`).

## 🔗 Role in the Ecosystem

PathwayZA serves as the primary entry point for students. Once a matching career pathway is established:

- **Unified Profile**: The student's subject list and grade level are securely stored.
- **Seamless Routing**: The platform exports this data to the **Certification Hub** (to close specific skill gaps) and the **Job Board** (to apply for entry-level vacancies) using a single, unified workflow.
